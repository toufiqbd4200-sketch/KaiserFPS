 Complete iOS Roblox Guide

1. The Secret iOS Weapon: Guided Access

Apple devices throttle games when background notifications or swipe gestures trigger. Guided Access gives Roblox 100% uninterrupted CPU/GPU core priority!

👉 Open Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access > Turn it ON. Inside Roblox, triple-click the Power button to start!

2. Hardware RAM Flush (Free 2GB+ Memory)

iOS keeps heavy apps frozen in RAM. To instantly purge all inactive memory before gaming:

👉 Hold Volume Up + Power until the slider appears. Then hold the AssistiveTouch Home button for 5 seconds until the screen flashes. Your RAM is now completely refreshed!

3. Turn OFF Low Power Mode

Low Power Mode cuts Apple A-series and M-series GPU clock frequencies by 50% and locks Roblox to a stuttering 30 FPS. Keep Low Power Mode OFF while gaming.

4. Roblox Metal FFlags & ClientAppSettings

iOS Roblox runs on Apple's Metal API. You can optimize rendering via Chevstrap/Voidstrap configuration files:

5. In-Game Roblox Settings

In Roblox Settings menu: Switch Graphics Mode from Automatic to Manual and set slider between 2–4 bars for smooth 60 FPS in PvP titles (Rivals, Bedwars, Arsenal).
